# Better Wheel

Press G and the item wheel opens on all your items, skipping the category wheel.

- Extra wheels for just food, just gear, or both. Up to four, each with its own key and item types.
- The wheel stays open, so you can eat three foods or change your gear in one go.
- Hold the key, point at an item and let go to use it. A quick tap opens the wheel for clicking.

Keys start unbound. Set them in [Configuration Manager](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/shudnal/ConfigurationManager/) (F1).
The other settings are there too, including one to bring back the category wheel.

Client-side only. Other players don't need it.
