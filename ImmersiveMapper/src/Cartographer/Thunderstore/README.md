# Cartographer

Draw your own maps in worlds without the map (the Immersive preset). Nothing is ever given to you:
no coordinates, no "you are here", no automatic pins. You measure, you sketch, you make the map.

## Map case (workbench)

- One item holds your **draft sheets** (3, up to 5 with sheet parts), a **tally** that counts your
  paces while you carry the case, and a **journal** of legs.
- **M** takes it out (in no-map worlds). **Left-click** shows the small map, **right-click** opens
  the full view to draw: charcoal, stamps, labels, eraser. Drafts stay rough on purpose.
- **J** ends a leg (notes the tally with your own label), **K** resets the tally, **F** flips sheets.
- In the drawing view: wheel zooms, middle mouse pans, **right-click selects** a thing to move,
  nudge, resize or delete, Ctrl+Z undoes, Esc closes.

## Cartography table

In no-map worlds the vanilla table becomes your drawing table, one player at a time:

- A **master map** that grows as you draw, with a grid at your own scale (paces per square).
- Quill with colours, a **wash** brush and **area fill** for biomes, dotted and straight lines,
  text and stamps in three sizes, solid or see-through.
- **Lay a draft over the master** like tracing paper, fit it (drag, turn, resize, or two-point
  fit), then copy it as is or trace it. Copied drafts are wiped for the next trip.
- Journal legs become **measuring strings** of true length: you only choose the direction.
- **Pigments:** a table knows lampblack; teach it each biome's colour once with something from that
  biome (raspberries, guck, freeze glands, ...).
- Leaving the table refreshes the copy of the master your case carries.

## Log line (forge)

Fit it to a ship's stern: it counts the distance sailed, in paces. End a leg aboard to note the run
as a sea leg for the table.
