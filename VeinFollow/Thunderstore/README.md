# Vein Follow

Mine a whole rock from one spot.

- The chunk you hit stays; each swing damages another chunk of the rock, the one farthest from you first. The chunk you hit goes last.
- A swing that breaks a chunk passes its spare damage on to the next.
- Loot drops in front of you, not inside the ground.
- Optional: the ground above buried chunks is dug out as they break.

Works on ore veins and every multi-part rock. Hold Left Alt to mine the normal way, or swap it so the key turns
this on. Settings are in [Configuration Manager](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/shudnal/ConfigurationManager/) (F1).

Client-side. Other players don't need it: the rock you mine is handled by your game.
