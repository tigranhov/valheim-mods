# Vein Follow

Keep mining one spot and the whole rock follows.

- A swing that breaks a chunk passes its leftover damage on to the nearest chunk left, so ore veins and big rocks come apart from where you stand.
- Swinging at the ground over buried chunks mines them instead of digging.
- Loot drops in front of you, not inside the ground.
- Optional: the ground above buried chunks is dug out as they break.

Hold Left Alt to mine the normal way, or swap it so the key turns following on. Settings are in
[Configuration Manager](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/shudnal/ConfigurationManager/) (F1).

Client-side. Other players don't need it: the rock you mine is handled by your game.
