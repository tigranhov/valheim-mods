# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Leftover damage from a swing carries on to the nearest chunk left, on ore veins and every multi-part rock.
- Swinging at the ground over buried chunks mines them.
- Loot drops in front of you (or where the chunk was).
- Optional digging of the ground above buried chunks.
- Hold a key to mine the normal way, or the other way round.
