# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- The chunk you hit stays and each swing damages another chunk of the rock, farthest from you first; the chunk you hit goes last.
- Spare damage from a swing that breaks a chunk carries on to the next.
- Works on ore veins and every multi-part rock.
- Loot drops in front of you (or where the chunk was).
- Optional digging of the ground above buried chunks.
- Hold a key to mine the normal way, or the other way round.
